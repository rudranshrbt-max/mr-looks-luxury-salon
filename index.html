Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Do I need to book an appointment or can I walk in?+

Walk-ins are welcome, but we highly recommend booking in advance, especially for bridal services, chemical treatments, and weekends. Booking guarantees your preferred time slot and stylist.

How far in advance should I book bridal services?+

For bridal makeup and packages, we recommend booking 2-3 months in advance, especially during wedding season (October-March). A trial session is included and scheduled 2-4 weeks before your wedding.

What is your cancellation policy?+

We require 24-hour notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee, especially for bridal and package bookings where advance payment has been made.

Do you offer home services?+

Yes, we offer premium home services for bridal makeup and party makeup within Indore city limits. Additional charges apply based on location. Contact us via WhatsApp for quotes.

What payment methods do you accept?+

We accept cash, all major credit/debit cards, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), and net banking. For bridal packages, we require 30-50% advance payment to confirm booking.

Are your products safe for sensitive skin?+

Yes. We use only premium, dermatologically-tested products from brands like L'Oréal Professionnel, Matrix, and Schwarzkopf. Please inform your stylist of any allergies or sensitivities before the service.

How long do keratin and smoothening treatments last?+

Keratin treatments typically last 3-5 months, while smoothening lasts 4-6 months, depending on hair type and aftercare. We provide detailed aftercare instructions and recommend sulfate-free products.

Is parking available?+

Yes, we have dedicated parking space for our clients right in front of the salon. We're located opposite Baskin Robbins in Patrakar Colony, easy to find and access.

Terms & Conditions

Please read carefully before booking.

1. Advance Booking

All appointments are subject to availability. Advance booking is highly recommended, especially for bridal and package services. Walk-ins are accommodated based on slot availability.

2. Cancellation Policy

We require a minimum of 24-hour notice for any cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee of up to 50% of the service value, particularly for bridal and premium services.

3. Advance Payment

An advance payment of 30–50% is required to confirm bookings for bridal services, chemical treatments (keratin, smoothening, botox), and special packages. Balance is payable on the day of service.

4. Refund Policy

No refunds will be provided on completed services. We ensure 100% satisfaction before you leave the salon. If you're not happy, speak to our manager immediately for resolution.

5. Privacy Policy

Your personal data and contact information are kept strictly confidential and will never be shared with third parties. We use your data only for appointment confirmations and service updates.

6. Salon Hygiene Policy

We maintain the highest standards of hygiene. All tools are sanitized between clients, single-use items are discarded after every use, and our staff follows strict hygiene protocols.

7. Punctuality

Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment. Late arrivals may result in reduced service time or rescheduling to accommodate other clients.

8. Pricing

All prices are subject to change without prior notice. Final pricing depends on hair length, thickness, and product quantity used. You'll be informed of the final price before service begins.
