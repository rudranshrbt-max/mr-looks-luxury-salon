Our Story

Crafted for the
Discerning Few

Mr. Looks Unisex Salon was born from a simple belief: every person deserves to look and feel extraordinary. Nestled in the heart of Patrakar Colony, Indore, we've spent over 8 years perfecting the art of grooming — blending modern techniques with timeless elegance.

From precision cuts to transformative colour treatments, our team of expert stylists bring international training and local artistry to every appointment. We don't just offer services — we deliver experiences that stay with you long after you leave.
