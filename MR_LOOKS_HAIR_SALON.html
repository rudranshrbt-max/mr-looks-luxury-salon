Good to Know

Salon Policies

These policies exist for one reason: so every guest gets the calm, careful experience we promise. They help us plan time fairly, keep the studio safe and hygienic, and protect both you and our team. A quick read now means no surprises later.

  • Advance booking is always recommended to secure your preferred time and stylist.
  • Walk-ins are warmly welcomed, subject to availability.
  • Please share accurate contact details so we can reach you about your appointment.
  • Confirmations may be sent by phone, SMS, WhatsApp, or email.

  • Please arrive 10–15 minutes before your scheduled time.
  • Late arrivals may mean a shorter service, to stay fair to the next guest's slot.
  • Significant delays may require your appointment to be rescheduled.
  • Full service quality can't be guaranteed once time has been shortened by a late arrival.

  • Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance wherever possible.
  • Repeated last-minute cancellations may require a deposit for future bookings.
  • Repeated no-shows may result in booking restrictions.

  • Premium, bridal, chemical, and longer-duration services may require an advance deposit.
  • Deposits become non-refundable if a cancellation falls outside the allowed window.
  • Any deposit requirement is communicated clearly at the time of booking.

Our services are professional and personalised — here's how we handle concerns fairly.

  • Services are generally non-refundable once completed.
  • If a genuine concern arises, please contact us within 48 hours of your appointment.
  • We review every concern and may offer a reasonable correction where appropriate.
  • Final refund decisions remain at salon management's discretion.

  • Your feedback genuinely helps us — we encourage you to share it.
  • Please raise any concern as soon as possible after your service.
  • We'll always make a reasonable effort to resolve a legitimate issue.

  • For everyone's safety, we ask that only clients receiving a service remain in the service area.
  • Children visiting with you should be supervised at all times.
  • The salon cannot be held responsible for accidents involving unsupervised children.

  • Please tell us about any allergies, skin sensitivities, medical conditions, or recent treatments before your service begins.
  • We reserve the right to decline a service that may pose a health risk.
  • Every tool and station is sanitised between clients to a high hygiene standard.

  • Results can vary depending on your hair's history and current condition.
  • Previous colour, box dye, or existing damage may affect the final outcome.
  • Some looks may safely need more than one session to achieve.

  • Bridal packages are built around a consultation and should be booked well in advance.
  • A deposit is required to secure your event date.
  • We strongly recommend a trial session ahead of the big day.

  • We may photograph completed work for our portfolio and marketing.
  • We'll always ask before publishing any photo that identifies you.
  • You're welcome to opt out of photography at any time — just let us know.

  • Please keep an eye on your personal belongings during your visit.
  • The salon cannot be held liable for items lost, stolen, or damaged on the premises.

  • We reserve the right to decline or end a service in cases of abusive behaviour, harassment, intoxication, safety concerns, or repeated policy violations.

  • Your information is used only for appointments, communication, and service records.
  • We never sell your personal information, and only share it with your consent or where legally required.
